02 YOUR ANONYMOUS RECORDS

Start with a blank collection.

The workshop template has 32 anonymous people, with 15 posts each. Give each post one category. Use P01–P32 instead of names or account handles, and leave identifying details out of your notes.

Your entries stay in this page. They are not sent or saved anywhere. Export a CSV before leaving; refreshing clears the records.

      03 COMPARE THE COUNTS

      Two labels, one limited view.

      Each complete person contributes one point. Both axes run from 0 to 15 posts. These are counts of the labels assigned to posts; they do not establish someone’s personality or identity.

      People with the same pair of counts share one bubble. A number inside a bubble is the number of people at that position.
