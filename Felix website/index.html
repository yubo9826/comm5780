01 A BIT ABOUT ME

Digital media,
with room to explore.

I’m a Digital Media postgraduate student at the University of Leeds, interested in how digital tools influence what we make, share and notice. AI video generation is one area I’m curious about: the images it produces, the choices behind them and the stories they can tell.

This site brings my studies together with a more personal record of life in Leeds. There are city walks, photographs from the Welsh coast and meals at home, alongside notes on building this space. R&B is usually somewhere in the background.
