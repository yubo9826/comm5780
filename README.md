# Felix's Digital Media Portfolio

A personal portfolio about Felix's Digital Media studies at the University of Leeds, creative interests, and everyday life in Leeds and beyond.

## Pages

- **Home** (`index.html`) — An introduction to Felix, current modules, topics of interest, and creative exploration.
- **Leeds** (`leeds.html`) — A student’s view of the University of Leeds campus, alongside everyday photos of the city and life at home.
- **Photo Journal** (`journal.html`) — A 32-photo diary arranged into three chapters: the Welsh coast, Leeds, and everyday moments.
- **Project Reflection** (`reflection.html`) — A weekly note on building the website, what Felix learned, and what he plans to improve.

## Features

- Responsive layouts for desktop and mobile screens.
- Photo galleries with category filters and an image viewer.
- A rotating feature on the home page and back-to-top controls.
- No build tools, package installation, or server-side code required.

## Built with

- HTML
- CSS
- Vanilla JavaScript

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a web browser. The site can also be served from this folder with any static web server.

Google Fonts and the University of Leeds campus-tour photos are loaded from external websites, so an internet connection is needed for those resources. Photos in `assets/` are stored locally.

## Images and credits

- Campus photos and building descriptions on the Leeds page are from the [University of Leeds self-guided campus tour](https://spotlight.leeds.ac.uk/campus-tour/).
- Everyday-life photos on the Leeds page and photos in the journal are Felix's personal photos.
